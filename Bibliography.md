# Bibliography and References

This bibliography compiles the academic literature, dataset documentations, core machine learning papers, and software library references that form the scientific and technical foundation for the **Machine Learning Feature Selection Benchmarking Platform**.

For convenience, references are formatted in both **APA 7th Edition** and **IEEE** styles, accommodating various academic and technical reporting guidelines.

---

## 1. Automated Data Preprocessing & AutoML

These references cover the theoretical models and current state-of-the-art in automating data preparation, cleaning, and preprocessing within machine learning pipelines.

### APA 7th Edition
*   Mladenovic, S., Lindauer, M., & Doerr, C. (2026). Automated data preparation for machine learning: A survey. *Journal of Data-centric Machine Learning Research (DMLR)*, 2026.
*   Salhi, A., Henslee, A. C., Ross, J., Jabour, J., & Dettwiller, I. (2023). Data preprocessing using AutoML: A survey. *arXiv preprint arXiv:2308.14041*. Presented at the 2023 Congress in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, & Applied Computing (CSCE).

### IEEE Style
*   [1] S. Mladenovic, M. Lindauer, and C. Doerr, "Automated data preparation for machine learning: A survey," *Journal of Data-centric Machine Learning Research (DMLR)*, 2026.
*   [2] A. Salhi, A. C. Henslee, J. Ross, J. Jabour, and I. Dettwiller, "Data preprocessing using AutoML: A survey," *arXiv preprint arXiv:2308.14041*, 2023.

## 2. Feature Selection Methodologies

These citations represent the foundational research and core mathematical formulations behind filter, wrapper, and embedded feature selection algorithms.

### APA 7th Edition
*   Breiman, L. (2001). Random forests. *Machine Learning*, 45(1), 5-32.
*   Guyon, I., Weston, J., Barnhill, S., & Vapnik, V. (2002). Gene selection for cancer classification using support vector machines. *Machine Learning*, 46(1-3), 389-422. *(Provides the algorithmic basis for Recursive Feature Elimination - RFE)*.
*   Kursa, M. B., & Rudnicki, W. R. (2010). Feature selection with the Boruta package. *Journal of Statistical Software*, 36(11), 1-13. *(Academic foundation for Boruta, proposed in Future Scope)*.
*   Tibshirani, R. (1996). Regression shrinkage and selection via the lasso. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B (Methodological)*, 58(1), 267-288. *(Defines L1 Regularization / LASSO feature selection)*.

### IEEE Style
*   [3] L. Breiman, "Random forests," *Machine Learning*, vol. 45, no. 1, pp. 5-32, 2001.
*   [4] I. Guyon, J. Weston, S. Barnhill, and V. Vapnik, "Gene selection for cancer classification using support vector machines," *Machine Learning*, vol. 46, no. 1-3, pp. 389-422, 2002.
*   [5] M. B. Kursa and W. R. Rudnicki, "Feature selection with the Boruta package," *Journal of Statistical Software*, vol. 36, no. 11, pp. 1-13, 2010.
*   [6] R. Tibshirani, "Regression shrinkage and selection via the lasso," *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B (Methodological)*, vol. 58, no. 1, pp. 267-288, 1996.
